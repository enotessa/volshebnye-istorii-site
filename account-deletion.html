Data controls

Delete an account and associated data

Updated October 1, 2026

An adult user can delete the account directly in the App. To prevent deletion of another person's account, the action is verified with a code sent to the linked email address.

Delete the account in the App

  1. Open Magic Stories and go to Premium or account management.
  2. Select account deletion.
  3. Request a verification code and enter the code sent to the linked email.
  4. Confirm permanent deletion.

After confirmation, active sessions are terminated and sign-in with the deleted account is no longer available.

If you cannot access the App

Email raccoon.labs.app@yandex.ru from the address linked to the account and use “Magic Stories account deletion” as the subject.

We may ask you to verify access to that mailbox. Never send a password, bank confirmation code, full card number, or CVC/CVV.

Data deleted

  • email address and internal account data;
  • active sessions and sign-in tokens;
  • Premium state and account-linked entitlements;
  • account history where retention is no longer required by law.

Data that may be retained

Payment, fiscal, and other records required for tax, refunds, disputes, or compliance are retained only to the extent and for the period required by law. They are not used to restore the deleted account.

Local device data

The child's name or nickname, detailed progress, favorites, and family voice recordings are stored locally. Delete recordings in the App, clear the App's data in Android settings, or uninstall the App to remove local data.

Effect of deletion

Account deletion is irreversible and may prevent restoration of purchased stories and Premium. If you only need a break, log out or disable analytics and advertising under Profile → Privacy.
