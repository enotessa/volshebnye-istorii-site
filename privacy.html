Data protection

Personal Data Processing and Privacy Policy

Updated September 19, 2026

In brief: accounts, payments, and support are for adults. The child's name, detailed progress, favorites, and family voice recordings remain on the device. The server receives data needed for email sign-in, purchases, and subscriptions. The free version uses limited analytics and advertising configured for a child-directed audience.

1. Scope

This Policy describes personal-data processing in the Magic Stories Android application, package com.volshebnye.istorii, the developer website, and the server features used for accounts, payments, subscriptions, and support.

The Policy is based primarily on Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ on Personal Data. It is a notice and does not itself replace a separate consent where applicable law requires one.

2. Data controller

The controller is Anastasia Maksimovna Pokamestova, a self-employed individual applying the Russian Professional Income Tax regime, Russian Taxpayer ID (INN) 695205887530, Russian Federation.

Send privacy requests to raccoon.app.labs@gmail.com.

3. Users and data processed

The account and payment data subject is an adult user, parent, or legal guardian. Depending on the features used, we may process:

  • account data: email address, internal user ID, one-time-code hashes, session identifiers and expiry times;
  • purchase data: selected service or story, amount, currency, date, status, payment ID, payment provider, refund and receipt information;
  • subscription data: plan, paid-period dates, renewal status, and the payment-method reference received from the payment provider;
  • technical data: IP address, request time and result, App version and distribution channel, device model, operating-system version, language, internal installation ID, and connectivity information;
  • notifications: device push token and notification delivery or opening events;
  • analytics: feature and screen events, story ID, reading mode, coarse progress milestones, ad and purchase events, and counts of local narrations without their content;
  • diagnostics: error type, exception class, App version, and limited technical details not intended to include story text, the child's name, email, or audio;
  • support: sender address, message, voluntarily attached files, and pre-filled technical details if the user chooses to send an email from their mail application.

We do not receive the full bank-card number, expiry date, CVC/CVV, or the user's email password. Card details are entered on the payment provider's protected page.

4. Purposes and legal grounds

  • Account creation, protection, code-based sign-in, and access restoration: steps requested by the user to enter into and perform the agreement and protect the service.
  • Payments, access delivery, subscriptions, refunds, and transaction history: entering into and performing the user agreement.
  • Receipts and mandatory records: compliance with tax and other legal obligations.
  • Prevention of duplicate requests, abuse, and fraud: the controller's lawful interests where the user's rights are not overridden.
  • Technical analytics, diagnostics, and App improvement: service reliability and lawful interests; separate consent is requested where required by applicable law.
  • Non-personalized or restricted advertising in the free version: operation of the free model selected by the user, with child-audience settings.
  • Support: responding to the user's request, performing the agreement, and protecting the parties' rights.

5. Data kept on the device

In the current App version, the child's name or nickname, selected character and grammatical gender, detailed reading progress, favorites, parent PIN, and family voice recordings are stored locally and are not uploaded to the controller's server.

Analytics may receive only coarse events indicating that a story was started, progressed, or completed and the number of local recordings. The child's name, the content of a user narration, and audio files are not sent to analytics. Local data can be removed in the App, by clearing App data, or by uninstalling the App.

6. Services and recipients

We may provide the minimum necessary data to:

  • Yandex Cloud — backend, database, digital-content hosting, and service email through Yandex Cloud Postbox;
  • YooKassa and Robokassa — payments, agreed recurring-payment references, refunds, and transaction status;
  • the Russian Federal Tax Service, My Tax, and Robokassa fiscalization — receipts where applicable;
  • myTracker — restricted product analytics in the RuStore build, with Kid Mode enabled and without setting the user's email or the child's name as analytics IDs;
  • Yandex Advertising Network — ad delivery and measurement in the free version; the App marks the audience as age-restricted, does not grant location consent, and removes the Android Advertising ID permission;
  • RuStore Push — service and informational notifications in the RuStore build;
  • Google Play — distribution and payments if the Google Play version is published;
  • Google Gmail and the user's mail provider — receipt and delivery of a support email sent at the user's choice; the relevant mail services process the message content;

The exact set depends on the App version, distribution channel, and payment method. Independent recipients also process data under their own policies and legal obligations.

7. Processing and storage location

Processing is primarily automated and may include collection, recording, organization, accumulation, storage, updating, retrieval, use, provision to authorized recipients, restriction, deletion, and destruction.

The main backend and account database are hosted in Yandex Cloud in the Russian Federation. A support request voluntarily sent to a Gmail address may be processed by Google using infrastructure outside Russia. Before systematic use of a foreign service, the controller completes localization and cross-border notification requirements or replaces that service with a Russian provider.

8. Retention

  • sign-in, email-change, and account-deletion codes are valid for 10 minutes; request-rate limits last up to one hour;
  • access tokens last up to 15 minutes and refresh tokens up to 90 days, or until logout, email change, account deletion, or revocation;
  • email, subscription state, and purchased-access data are kept while the account exists and purchase restoration is required;
  • after confirmed account deletion, email, active sessions, account history, and entitlements are deleted or detached, and renewal is stopped;
  • payment and fiscal records required for tax, refunds, disputes, and proof of a lawful transaction are retained for the periods required by law;
  • support correspondence is retained until the request is resolved and afterward as reasonably needed to protect the parties' rights;
  • analytics and advertising data are retained according to the relevant service settings and periods, then deleted or aggregated.

9. Security

Measures include access control, HTTPS, hashing of one-time codes and tokens, attempt limits, server-side payment verification, event minimization, and other reasonable organizational and technical safeguards. Payment and backend secrets are not bundled into the App.

No transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security. If an incident is discovered, the controller acts in accordance with applicable law.

10. User rights and account deletion

Subject to applicable law, users may request information, correction, restriction, or deletion of unlawfully processed or excessive data, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and complain to the competent authority or a court.

Email management, logout, and account deletion are available on the in-App account screen. Requests may also be sent to raccoon.app.labs@gmail.com. Access to the linked email may be required to prevent deletion of another person's account.

Withdrawal does not stop processing required by law or an existing agreement. Account deletion does not remove records that must be retained by law and may make earlier purchases impossible to restore.

11. Children's data

The App is designed for shared use by a child and an adult. Registration, email entry, payments, subscription management, support requests, and acceptance of legal terms are for an adult user, parent, or legal guardian only.

We do not knowingly ask a child to provide contact or payment data independently. Parents should use a nickname and avoid sensitive information in voice recordings or support messages.

12. Changes and contact

The current version is published on this page. Material changes will be communicated by an available method. Contact raccoon.app.labs@gmail.com.
