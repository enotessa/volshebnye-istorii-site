Subscription, explained

Premium Subscription Terms

Effective September 18, 2026

RUB 199 per month. The user confirms the first payment. Later charges occur automatically only after separate consent to auto-renewal and saving the payment method.

What Premium includes

  • access to the entire available digital-story library;
  • reading and listening without advertising;
  • available family narration features;
  • restoring the subscription on another device using the verified email address.

Price and period

One period costs RUB 199. A period is one calendar month and begins after successful payment is confirmed. Before payment, the App displays the total amount and the expected next renewal date.

Automatic renewal

When auto-renewal is enabled, RUB 199 is charged at the end of each paid period. The user makes the first payment manually. Later charges use the saved payment method without requiring the details again.

The user consents to recurring charges in a separate action before the first payment. A subscription with auto-renewal is not created without this consent.

A reminder is sent to the verified email address approximately three days before renewal. If it is filtered by the email provider or the address is outdated, the terms and renewal date remain available in the App.

How to stop future charges

  1. Open the App.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Premium.
  4. Select Turn off auto-renewal and confirm.

Premium remains active until the end of the paid period. No new monthly charge is created. If a bank request was already submitted before cancellation was processed, the App will show that the result is still being verified.

If a charge fails

If the bank declines the charge or the saved payment method is unavailable, no new period is activated and auto-renewal may be disabled. Reading progress and user narrations are not deleted. The user may subscribe again.

Support

If money was charged but Premium did not activate, do not pay again. Email raccoon.app.labs@gmail.com with the account email, date, amount, and any available payment number.
