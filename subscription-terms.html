Subscription, explained

Premium Subscription Terms

Effective September 30, 2026

RUB 199 for one calendar month. The user initiates and confirms each paid period manually. There is no automatic renewal or charge without a new confirmation.

What Premium includes

  • access to the entire available digital-story library;
  • reading and listening without advertising;
  • available family narration features;
  • restoring the subscription on another device using the verified email address.

Price and period

One period costs RUB 199. A period is one calendar month and begins after successful payment is confirmed. Before each payment, the App displays the total amount and access term.

Manual renewal

Premium does not renew automatically. At the end of the paid period, Premium access ends unless the user purchases another period.

To continue access, the user opens Premium in the App, checks the price and term, and confirms a new payment. Payment details are not used for recurring charges.

If a new payment fails

If the bank declines the payment or the payment service does not confirm it, no new period is activated. Reading progress and user narrations are not deleted. Premium may be purchased again.

Support

If money was charged but Premium did not activate, do not pay again. Email raccoon.labs.app@yandex.ru with the account email, date, amount, and any available payment number.
