Payments and requests

Cancellation and Refunds

Effective September 18, 2026

To stop future Premium charges, use subscription management in your profile. To request a refund of a specific payment, send a separate request to support.

Turning off auto-renewal

In the App, open Profile → Premium → Turn off auto-renewal. Access remains active until the end of the paid period, and no new charges are created.

Turning off auto-renewal does not by itself cancel a service already provided or automatically refund the current paid period.

When you may request a refund

You may submit a request, including when:

  • money was charged but paid access was not provided;
  • there was a confirmed duplicate charge for the same service;
  • a payment was made after timely cancellation of auto-renewal and was not part of a bank request already initiated;
  • the service was unavailable due to the Provider;
  • a refund is required on another ground provided by the laws of the Russian Federation.

How to submit a request

Email raccoon.app.labs@gmail.com from the address linked to the purchase, or state that address in your message.

Include:

  • the purchased story title or Premium;
  • payment date and amount;
  • the payment identifier, if shown;
  • a brief explanation;
  • a screenshot of the payment status if needed.

Never send your full card number, expiry date, CVC/CVV, password, SMS code, or push-confirmation code.

Review and payment

The Provider verifies the payment and access information and responds within the period required by Russian law, generally no later than 10 calendar days after receiving sufficient information to review the request.

An approved refund is issued through the payment service to the original payment method. The bank and payment system determine when the funds arrive. Access granted by the refunded purchase may be revoked after a full refund.

Purchase of one story

A single-story purchase is not a subscription and creates no recurring charges. A refund request is assessed individually, taking into account when digital access was provided, actual use, and applicable law.

Disputed or unknown status

If a payment is being verified, do not pay again before support replies. This helps prevent duplicate charges. The Provider will compare the payment with the payment-service records and report the result.
