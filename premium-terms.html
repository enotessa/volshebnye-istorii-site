2. Definitions

App means the Magic Stories Android application with package identifier com.volshebnye.istorii.

Digital Story means a collection of digital materials available in the App that may include text, illustrations, audio, and interactive elements.

Account means the user record identified by a verified email address and used to grant and restore purchased access.

Premium means paid access to the entire available story library and ad-free use of the App during the paid period.

3. Service

The Provider grants the user remote access to the selected digital content and App features, and the user pays for the selected service.

One story — RUB 99

Access to the selected story without a time limit imposed by the Provider and without recurring charges.

Premium — RUB 199/month

Access to the entire available library without ads, with automatic monthly renewal.

Before payment, the App displays the service name, total price, access period, and whether automatic renewal applies. If the price shown in the payment interface differs from this page, the payment-interface price applies and the user may decline to pay.

The Provider applies the Russian Professional Income Tax regime. VAT does not apply. An electronic receipt is delivered by email or another method supported by the payment service and applicable law.

4. Granting access

Access is granted after the Provider receives reliable confirmation of successful payment from the payment service. Returning from the payment page does not itself confirm payment.

The purchase is linked to the email address supplied and verified by the user. To restore access on another device, the user must sign in with the same address. The user is responsible for the address being correct and for retaining access to that mailbox.

If access does not appear within a reasonable time after a charge, do not pay again. Contact support and provide the email address, date, amount, and any available payment identifier.

An internet connection may be required to operate the App, download content initially, verify a purchase, and restore access. Previously downloaded content may remain available offline subject to the App's technical capabilities and the current paid period.

5. Premium and automatic renewal

The first paid Premium period is one calendar month from payment confirmation. If the user separately consents to saving the payment method for later charges, the subscription automatically renews for another month at the end of the current period.

Each renewal costs RUB 199. The Provider sends a reminder to the verified email address approximately three days before the charge. The user is responsible for keeping the address current and monitoring subscription status.

Future charges can be disabled in the App under Profile → Premium → Turn off auto-renewal. Access remains active until the end of the paid period. Turning off auto-renewal does not automatically refund the current period.

If a renewal request was sent to the bank before cancellation was processed, it may be technically impossible to cancel that request. Later renewals will still be disabled, and the result of the initiated payment will appear after confirmation by the payment service.

If a renewal charge fails, no new paid period is granted and auto-renewal may be disabled. The user may subscribe again.

A concise explanation is available in the Subscription Terms.
